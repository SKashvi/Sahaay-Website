## What Sahaay is

- Sahaay is an AI sales agent for online stores (D2C e-commerce brands). It sits on the store as a chat widget.
- It greets shoppers, answers questions about the store's products, recommends what fits, adds items to the cart, and handles order questions after purchase.
- It is built India-first (prices in rupees, delivery checks by PIN code) and works for online stores anywhere.
- Sahaay has not launched yet. Brands can join the waitlist on this page.
- सहाय (sahaay) means "help".

## Before purchase

- **Speaks first.** Sahaay can open the conversation when a shopper shows a signal. The store chooses the triggers:
  - Exit intent: the cursor heads for the close tab or back button.
  - Time on page: the shopper lingers on a product without acting.
  - Scroll depth: the shopper scrolls down to details such as delivery and returns.
  - Cart value: the cart crosses a threshold the store sets.
- **Answers product questions** about sizes, colours, fabric, stock, delivery and returns, from the store's own catalog and policies, usually in under 2 seconds.
- **Recommends products** and shows product cards with size and colour pickers inside the chat.
- **Add to cart in chat, checkout on the store.** Shoppers add to cart inside the chat, then go to the store's own checkout page to pay. Sahaay does not take payments or run its own checkout.
- **Cart recovery.** Contextual nudges that know what is in the cart and why the shopper paused (for example a sizing doubt or a delivery question).

## After purchase (Shopify stores only)

- **Order lookup.** Shoppers find their orders by email with a six-digit verification code, or by order ID, and see their order history.
- **Tracking.** "Where's my order?" gets the order's status in the chat.
- **Returns, refunds and cancellations.** Shoppers report an issue in the chat (wrong size, damaged item, not as described, changed my mind) and can attach a photo. Damage photos get an AI score to help verify the claim.
- **The store approves every action.** Refunds, returns and cancellations wait in the store's admin dashboard until a person approves them. Nothing executes on its own.
- About 80% of post-purchase questions are resolved without a human.

## For the store (merchant admin)

- Admin sections: orders, returns and cancellations, products (catalog), inventory, knowledge base (policies such as shipping, returns, sizing, fabric care), widget settings, branding and widget theme, offers and discount codes, requests, bundles, and conversations.
- An analytics dashboard shows conversations, saved carts and the revenue Sahaay brought in.
- Sahaay only answers from the store's own catalog and policies, in the store's brand voice. Updating a knowledge base entry changes the answers on the next chat.

## Setup

- The store shares its store link. Sahaay learns the catalog, policies and brand voice in under 24 hours. No scripts or decision trees to write.
- The store adds one snippet to the site and Sahaay goes live.
